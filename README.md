## ORIVEXY Validator

**Before you pay, check every IBAN belongs to the right person.**
It matches your payment batch against the bank's account ownership certificate and flags, payment by payment,
whether the account is correct, belongs to someone else, or is missing. 100 % local: no bank data leaves your server or computer.

**Antes de pagar, comprueba que cada IBAN es de quien dice ser.** Cruza tu remesa con el certificado de titularidad
del banco y marca cada pago como correcto, de otro titular o sin certificado. Todo en local.
